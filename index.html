A little about the academy

A community behind the game.

Gelvandale Rugby Academy is a community-centred rugby development effort in Gqeberha. Its public story connects rugby with discipline, education, exposure and the belief that young people deserve opportunities beyond the field.

Public reports describe the academy’s work, tours and support from people and organisations around it. We link to those reports rather than add unverified claims or totals.

“My academy is there to show children that life has more to offer than what is now happening in our communities.”Enrico Grootboom · The Herald · October 2025
