GELVANDALE RUGBY ACADEMY · GQEBERHA

“Not all children are lost to crime” — Enrico Grootboom's vision is to show young people that life can offer more.

The academy uses rugby as a platform for development, mentorship, discipline, education and exposure. The goal is bigger than a result on the field: it is helping young people recognise what may be possible next.
