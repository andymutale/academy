# Media sources and temporary replacement strategy

## Current production-facing imagery

The photographs and video clips shown in the site are temporary Pexels stock placeholders. They are explicitly labelled as illustrative and are not presented as photographs or footage of Gelvandale Rugby Academy. Original imagery from the academy should replace these assets when supplied and approved.

The canonical list of temporary stock assets is in [image-url-manifest.txt](image-url-manifest.txt). The site intentionally keeps the academy crest/brand mark separate from photography and does not replace it with stock content.

## News and public reporting

External article/source links may remain attached to the news copy for research and verification, but the page thumbnails themselves use stock photography. No stock photo should be captioned or interpreted as the actual image from the linked article.

## Homepage motion sequence

The scroll-led homepage film strip reuses Pexels images 3679338 and 8586346 plus muted inline previews for Pexels video IDs 38212182, 38212185 and 37643420. These remain temporary stock placeholders and are not footage of academy sessions. Video files are remote MP4 streams; local copies are not included in the project archive.
